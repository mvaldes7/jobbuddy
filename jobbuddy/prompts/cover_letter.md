# Cover letter in the candidate's voice (queue: tailor)

Write a role-specific cover letter in the candidate's own voice. Study the STYLE SAMPLE for tone,
rhythm, sentence length, and structure, but **never copy its company-specific content**.

## Structure (4-5 short paragraphs, 300-420 words)

1. **Opening.** Name the exact role and company, then give the candidate's personal hook about why
   healthcare and technology matter to him. Use ONLY the PERSONAL HOOK below (you may lightly rephrase
   it). Never invent personal or medical details.
2. **Why this company.** Connect the hook to something specific in the job posting.
3. **Proof.** Give 2-3 concrete accomplishments with metrics, taken from the profile bullets. Emphasize
   "built it myself" ownership, for example personally building the validated mobile app.
4. **Fit.** Map his experience honestly to the role's top 2-3 requirements. Do not claim tools or domains
   that are not in the profile.
5. **Close.** End warmly and directly: thank them, invite a conversation, sign off with "Sincerely," and
   then his full name (`profile.full_name`).

Hard rules: every number must appear in the profile. Use no placeholders such as [Company]. Write plain
text only, with paragraphs separated by a blank line, and avoid heavy em-dash use. The validator rejects
unknown numbers, unknown proper nouns, and tools that are not in the profile. It also rejects job
requirements phrased as first-person claims.
