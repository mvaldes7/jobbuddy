# Cover letter tailored from the candidate's own letter (queue: tailor)

The BASE LETTER below is Mike's own cover letter. It is both the source content and the format: every
job's letter starts from this letter's text and is tailored to the role. Keep his voice, wording,
paragraph structure and order wherever they still fit; change only what the new role requires.

## How to tailor (keep the base letter's 5-paragraph shape, 300-430 words)

1. **Opening.** Keep the base letter's personal hook (healthcare experience; "less of an industry to
   optimize..."). Swap in the exact role title and company, and replace the old company-specific
   sentences with one tied to something specific in this job posting. Never invent personal or medical
   details beyond the base letter and the PERSONAL HOOK.
2. **Background (BioFortis).** Keep largely as written; adjust emphasis to the role.
3. **Proof.** Keep the "built it myself" story and the metrics from the base letter. Swap or reorder
   proof points from the profile bullets so the ones most relevant to this role lead.
4. **Why now / why this company.** Keep the "After eight years..." framing; retarget it to this company's
   mission and the role's top 2-3 requirements, honestly. Remove references to the base letter's original
   company (and anything only true for it).
5. **Close.** Keep his closing; sign off with "Sincerely," and then his full name (`profile.full_name`).
   Greeting: "Dear Hiring Team," unless the posting names the hiring manager.

Hard rules: every number must appear in the profile or the base letter. Use no placeholders such as
[Company]. Write plain text only, with paragraphs separated by a blank line (the text is poured into the
base letter's .docx template, which supplies the header, fonts, colors and layout, and is always uploaded
as a PDF). Avoid heavy em-dash use. The validator rejects unknown numbers, unknown proper nouns, and tools
that are not in the profile. It also rejects job requirements phrased as first-person claims.
